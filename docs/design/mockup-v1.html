YouTube, redesigned.

A Material 3 Expressive concept built on the Castii + Ledgii design language: seamless headers, a floating nav capsule with a centered create button, titled section cards, wallpaper color, and a mini-player that morphs into the watch screen. Tap things in the first phone; it's interactive.

YouThe Ledgii balance card becomes "watch time this week," with the same big-number and split-bar treatment.

Header

Seamless and opaque. Same surface as the page, 31pt heavy title at −1.3 tracking, two circular 44pt actions. No divider strip, gradient or border. Content scrolls under it.

Navigation

One floating capsule, 78pt tall and 48pt radius. A 60pt tonal pill springs between tabs, and the centered brand-red Create button replaces the old Shorts/upload slot. Notifications move into an Inbox tab.

Color

Wallpaper first. Every surface, container and outline comes from the seed. YouTube red is kept only for brand moments: the mark, Create, New/Live badges and progress. Outlines use the soft 87% / 32% tint.

Shape

28pt cards, 22pt thumbnails, full pills. Channel avatars use M3 Expressive shapes (cookie, clover, sunny). Selected chips and buttons round up when they change state.

Motion

Fade + rise between tabs with no crossfade, and a 0.94 press-scale with a spring back. The player is a container transform from the mini-player with a slow start, one soft overshoot and morphing corners, using only transform and clip.
