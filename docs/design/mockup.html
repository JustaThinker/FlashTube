Skipii: a YouTube player, not a YouTube app.

Round two of the Material 3 Expressive concept, built for a no-account client (NewPipeExtractor + Media3). Playback controls now sit on the video, the Create button is gone, and everything that needed a Google account is replaced by local features: subscriptions, history, downloads, audio-only, and SponsorBlock. Tap things in the first phone; it's interactive.

WALLPAPER
Home · interactiveTap the mini-player to open the player, then drag it down to minimize it. Tap the video to hide or show its controls.
WatchControls, the current chapter and the yellow sponsor segment all sit on the video. Swipe the player down to minimize it.
LibraryAll local: history, playlists, downloads, Takeout import and SponsorBlock settings.

Player

Controls belong on the video. The collapse arrow, quality, captions and settings sit at the top, skip and play/pause in the middle, and time, the current chapter, Skip sponsor, fullscreen and the chapter scrubber at the bottom. Chapters take no space below the video. Tap the video to hide them. Play/pause changes shape: a squircle while playing, a circle when paused.

Navigation

Four tabs, no Create button. Home, Subscriptions, Search, Library. Search becomes a tab because, without an account feed, searching is a main way in. The Subscriptions badge counts new uploads found by a local refresh.

Honest to the client

No Google sign-in, so no fake account features. Inbox, likes, dislikes and the custom feed are gone. Subscriptions, history, playlists and downloads are stored on the phone. Comments are read-only.

Castii DNA

Skip the sales pitch. Sponsor segments show up yellow on the scrubber and thumbnails, and get skipped automatically. Audio-only plays in the background through the same Media3 setup Castii uses.

Motion

Same rules as before. Fade + rise between tabs, 0.94 press-scale, and the mini-player grows into the video. Dragging the player down tracks the finger 1:1, then continues into the mini-player from wherever it was let go. The overlay controls fade in once the video lands, so they never animate while it's still moving.
