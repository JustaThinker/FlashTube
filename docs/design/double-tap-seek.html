Double-tap to seek

Double-click the left or right side of a video, then keep clicking to add 10 s per tap. One click on a side (or anywhere in the middle) still shows the controls. Same behaviour in the portrait player and in fullscreen; nothing on the mini-player.

A · Edge wash The familiar one. A soft half-moon of light swells in from the edge you tapped, three chevrons run in the seek direction, and "10 seconds" rolls up to 20, 30… with every extra tap.
B · Pop pill The Skipii one. A glass pill with the brand's fast-forward glyph springs out on that side. Every tap squishes it, throws sparks, rolls "+10 s" up, and nudges the picture a few pixels the way you're going. The time you'll land on sits under the pill.

Gesture, both concepts

  • Zones: left 40% seeks back, right 40% forward. A tap in the middle 20% only toggles the controls, with no waiting.
  • Burst: a double tap starts it. Each further single tap on the same side within 0.6 s adds 10 s, and the label shows the running total. It ends 0.6 s after the last tap.
  • Starting a burst hides the controls if they're showing. The thin line along the bottom shows where you'll land.
  • Cost: one tap on a side shows the controls 300 ms late (Android's double-tap timeout), the same as YouTube.

A · Edge wash: details

The wash is white at 13–26% on an elliptical half-moon that sizes off the video's height, so it stays in proportion in fullscreen. It enters with scaleX 0.55 → 1 from the edge (380 ms, emphasized), and each tap gives the chevrons a fresh wave (110 ms stagger). There's no ripple ring, because Skipii doesn't use tap ripples. Quiet, and anyone coming from YouTube reads it instantly.

B · Pop pill: details

The pill uses the controls' glass (black 42%, no blur, per 0.5.1). It springs in from 0.4×, and each tap does a squish (1.14 × 0.88), a glyph dash and 8 sparks in white and brand red (the same burst as the like Pop in Playful.kt). The frame nudge is ±5 px with a 3% scale, so the black edge never shows. With reduced motion there's no nudge and no sparks, just a fade.

How it would be built

detectTapGestures(onTap, onDoubleTap) on the VideoContent box, plus a SeekBurst state (side, totalMs, lastTapAt) that catches the follow-up single taps. It calls state.seekBy once per tap. Everything is drawn with graphicsLayer alpha and scale (rules 2 and 3), and any spring keeps the 0.0005 threshold. Optional: a light haptic tick on each +10.
